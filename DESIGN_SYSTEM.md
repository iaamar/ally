# My Perfect UI Rules

## 1. Overall Style
- I love **minimal and clean** designs.
- Not too much stuff on screen.
- Dark colors are best (black/gray background).
- Use simple fonts (no fancy curly letters).

## 2. Colors (My Favorite Colors)
- Background: very dark gray
- Cards: slightly lighter gray
- Main accent color: bright blue or cyan
- Good things: green
- Warnings: orange
- Bad things: red
- Text: white or light gray

## 3. How Things Should Look
- Make everything easy to read.
- Use plenty of space between things (but not too much).
- Cards should have soft shadows.
- Buttons should be simple rectangles.
- Charts and graphs should be clean and not crowded.

## 4. Observability Rules (Important for Dashboards)
- Show the most important numbers big at the top.
- Use small line charts (sparklines) next to numbers.
- Tables should be easy to scan.
- Show status with colors (green = good, red = bad).
- Filters and time pickers should be easy to find.

## 5. Things I Don't Like
- Too many bright colors
- Fake 3D effects
- Too many icons
- Crowded screens
- Slow animations

## 6. Examples I Like
- I like how Linear.app looks (clean and fast)
- I like Vercel dashboard
- I like simple parts of Grafana

## 7. Always Remember
When making any UI:
1. Make it simple first
2. Show important info clearly
3. Use my colors
4. Keep it minimal
